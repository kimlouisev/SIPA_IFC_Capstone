    1. Insurance Program Structure and Providers

    The Assessment will begin with a series of qualitative questions that will 1) identify what the insurance program aims to achieve and set the baseline for evaluating responses throughout the assessment, and 2) gather background of the Providers

    Questions

    a. Structure of the Insurance Program

    These questions will set the stage for the insurance requirements of the contracting entity. Before proceeding with the rest of the assessment tool, these questions should be answered by the contracting entity for the insurance. This will ground the discussion and help in assessment of some of the quantitative questions in this tool.

    • What is the insurance product meant to cover?
      Potential answers:
      - Protect farmers from catastrophic events
      - Provide guarantees for loans
      - Cover the cost of inputs
      - Other?

    • How is the insurance program funded/structured?
      Potential answers
      - National insurance program with government subsidy
      - Contract farming with a food manufacturer where payment for insurance gets taken out of farmers' earnings post-harvest
      - Agri-input supplier bundles inputs with insurance for a discounted/subsidized price

    • Will there be subsidies for the insurance product?
      If yes, what percentage of the premium will be covered?
      Who will provide the subsidy (government, donors, agribusinesses, financial institutions)?

    • Who are the intended beneficiaries of the insurance?
      Potential answers: smallholder farmers, farmer cooperatives, individual commercial farmers

    • Which regions and crops will the insurance program cover?

    • What is the proposed index-insurance type? Is it weather-based, area-yield, or a hybrid?

    b. Providers background

    These questions will give an overall introduction to the background and structure of the bidding insurance companies.

    • Who is doing the evaluation?
      e.g., government, agribusiness, agri-input suppliers, broker, etc.

    • What is the company’s core mission and experience in agricultural insurance?

    • What is the company's ownership structure/type?
      Potential answers: Private, Non-profit, Donor-backed, Government-supported

    • What regions or countries does the company operate in?

    • Who are its primary customers?

    • What is the proposed index-insurance type? Is it weather-based, area-yield, or a hybrid?
